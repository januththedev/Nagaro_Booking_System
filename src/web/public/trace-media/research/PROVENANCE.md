## Selection and rights notes

- Safest premium set: `trace-spaces-hero.webp`, `expert-city-industry-visits.webp`, `expert-city-coworking.webp`, `expert-city-private-offices.webp`, `creator-space.jpg`, `bay-07.jpg`, and `third-place-lobby.jpg`.
- `third-place-lobby.jpg` and `expert-city-food-hub.jpg` are byte-for-byte duplicates.
- `i-space.jpg` and `neo-hub.jpg` carry visible third-party credits. The TRACE site publishes them, but a production launch should preserve the credits or confirm a clean licensed original with TRACE.
- The current Bay 07 paragraph on the source page discusses technology-skills programs rather than venue specifications. Do not repeat it as a venue claim and do not invent capacity/pricing.
- The official footer links Facebook `TRACE.SL.info`, Instagram `trace_srilanka`, X `trace_lk`, and LinkedIn `trace-srilanka`. No social-only images were downloaded because the official site already supplied full-resolution originals with clearer provenance.
